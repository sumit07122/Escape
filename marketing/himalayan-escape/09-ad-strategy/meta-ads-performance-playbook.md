## 1. Audience Segmentation & Student Psychology

### Segment 1: The Hostel Squad Leader ("The Trip Planner")
- **Profile:** 2nd–4th year GLA University student who manages WhatsApp groups, initiates outing plans, and convinces friends to travel together.
- **Pain Point:** Everyone agrees in the chat, but when it's time to collect money, people back out or delay. Planning logistics (cabs, hotels, bus tickets) is exhausting.
- **Trigger Message:** *"Stop chasing payments in 4 different UPI apps. Share the link, let everyone lock their own seat for ₹1,000, and we take care of the entire 6D/5N route from GLA campus."*
- **Primary Creative:** Friends / Group Trip Poster & Squad Booking Walkthrough Carousel.

### Segment 2: The Routine-Burnout Student ("Exam & Assignment Fatigue")
- **Profile:** Engineering, Management, and BCA students overwhelmed with continuous assessments, 75% attendance rules, lab manuals, and campus monotony.
- **Pain Point:** Mental fatigue; feeling like college is passing by in dull timetables.
- **Trigger Message:** *"Same 8 AM alarm. Same cafeteria routine. Same lecture halls. You didn't come to college just to count attendance. Campus OFF. Mountains ON."*
- **Primary Creative:** Hero Campaign Poster & High-Emotion Mountain Reel.

### Segment 3: The Mountain Aesthetic & Experience Seeker
- **Profile:** Active on Instagram, saves mountain reels, loves cozy cafes, snow photography, Parvati Valley vibes, and stargazing.
- **Pain Point:** Wants an aesthetic, safe, and cinematic trip with good friends without booking shady, uncomfortable budget tours.
- **Trigger Message:** *"Stop saving Himachal reels. Wake up to pine-scented mornings in Old Manali and misty riverside strolls in Kasol. 6 Days / 5 Nights of cinematic memories."*
- **Primary Creative:** Manali × Kasol Destination Reveal & Carousel Guide.

### Segment 4: The Budget-Conscious / Parent-Approval Traveller
- **Profile:** Wants to travel but needs total financial transparency to budget allowances or ask parents for permission.
- **Pain Point:** Hidden charges, sudden driver allowances, sketchy booking portals with no verified confirmation.
- **Trigger Message:** *"₹6,200 flat. No asterisks. No hidden fees. Direct luxury coach departure right from GLA University campus. Lock your seat with ₹1,000 reservation deposit; balance payable before departure."*
- **Primary Creative:** ₹6,200 All-Inclusive Pricing Poster & Transparent Cost Breakdown.

## 3. Meta Ads Campaign Architecture

### Campaign 1: Top-of-Funnel (TOF) — High-Impact Engagement & Reach
- **Objective:** Engagement / Video Views (Instagram Reels & Feed Carousel)
- **Targeting:**
  - Location: Mathura (GLA University pin + 15km radius) + NCR student hubs (Agra, Aligarh, Delhi NCR).
  - Demographics: Age 18–25.
  - Interests: Backpacking, Himachal Pradesh, Manali, Trekking, College Life, Adventure Travel.
- **Recommended Test Budget:** ₹500/day
- **Creatives:**
  - Reel Ad 1: "The Group Chat That Finally Made It"
  - Carousel Ad: "Why Manali × Kasol Hits Different When You're 20"
  - Static Ad: Hero "Campus Off. Mountains On."

### Campaign 2: Middle-of-Funnel (MOF) — Traffic & Itinerary Consideration
- **Objective:** Traffic / Landing Page Views (Optimized for mobile `https://himalaya-escape.vercel.app/book`)
- **Targeting:** Custom Audience (Engaged with IG Profile last 30 days) + GLA Student Lookalike (1-2%).
- **Recommended Test Budget:** ₹700/day
- **Creatives:**
  - Carousel Ad: "Everything Covered for ₹6,200 (6 Days / 5 Nights)"
  - Static Ad: "Reserve with ₹1,000 Today — Balance at Departure"
  - Video Ad: "Step-by-Step Campus Pickup to Snow Peaks"

### Campaign 3: Bottom-of-Funnel (BOF) — Retargeting & Direct Booking
- **Objective:** Conversions (Initiate Checkout / Deposit Payment)
- **Targeting:**
  - Custom Audience: Website visitors (last 14 days) who haven't completed reservation.
  - Video Viewers: Watched >50% of TOF Reels.
- **Recommended Test Budget:** ₹400/day
- **Creatives:**
  - Static Ad: Coach Seating Blueprint ("Pick Your Window/Aisle Seat")
  - Static Ad: "Direct Bus Departure from GLA Campus Gate"
  - Video Ad: Real Booking Portal Walkthrough

## 5. Budget Allocation & Scaling Rules

- **Total Test Phase Budget:** ₹1,600 / day (₹11,200 / week)
- **Allocation:**
  - 50% (₹800) TOF: Generating fresh eyeballs across GLA campus.
  - 30% (₹480) MOF: Driving qualified landing page views to the booking portal.
  - 20% (₹320) BOF: Retargeting warm leads to close the ₹1,000 deposit.
- **Scaling Guardrails:**
  - **Rule 1:** If an ad set achieves Cost Per Outbound Click (CPC) < ₹4.50 and Click-Through Rate (CTR) > 2.2%, increase budget by 20% every 48 hours.
  - **Rule 2:** If an ad set has zero booking initiations after 200 clicks, pause creative and swap headline with a higher-clarity pricing hook.
  - **Rule 3:** Never edit an active winning ad directly; duplicate the ad set to preserve learning algorithm performance.
