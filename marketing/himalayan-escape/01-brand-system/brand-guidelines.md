## 3. Typography & Hierarchy

1. **Display & Campaign Headlines:**
   - *Font:* Syne / Clash Display / Manrope ExtraBold (Geometric, bold, cinematic)
   - *Characteristics:* High x-height, tight tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.03em`), uppercase for taglines and punchy hooks.
2. **Body & Technical Copy:**
   - *Font:* Manrope / Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter Medium
   - *Characteristics:* Crisp readability on small 360px mobile screens, generous line-height (`1.5x`), tabular numerals for transparent pricing (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
3. **Micro-Labels & Meta Badges:**
   - *Font:* Manrope SemiBold Uppercase
   - *Characteristics:* Wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.15em`), font sizes 10px–13px, paired with micro-icons.

## 4. Imagery & Art Direction Standards

- **Authentic Himalayan Photography:** Only imagery from Manali (Solang Valley, Old Manali, Atal Tunnel, Hadimba), Kasol (Parvati River valley, Chalal bridge, pine trails), Manikaran Sahib, and Tosh.
- **Cinematic Lighting:** Golden hour sun rays through cedar trees, misty riverbeds, blue hour mountain ridges, starlit mountain night skies.
- **Human Connection:** Real student silhouettes looking out over vast valleys, friends bundled in jackets around campfires, laughter on scenic coach drives.
- **Strict Avoidances:**
  - ❌ No cheap cartoon vectors or generic clip-art buses.
  - ❌ No chaotic neon discount splashes or artificial "Hurry! 2 Seats Left" banners.
  - ❌ No low-contrast text overlays without proper contrast backings.
  - ❌ No illegible decorative cursive fonts.

Every piece of advertising copy and creative MUST adhere to these confirmed business rules:

1. **Total Expedition Price:** Strictly **₹6,200 per person** (all-inclusive: luxury coach transit from GLA University, stays, meals, local guidance).
2. **Reservation Lock:** Strictly **₹1,000 per person** to secure a seat today.
3. **Gate Balance:** Strictly **₹5,200 per person**, payable before boarding as per confirmed payment schedule.
4. **Origin & Departure Point:** GLA University Campus, Mathura (direct university pickup).
5. **Destination Pair:** Manali × Kasol (with Solang Valley, Manikaran Sahib & Parvati Valley exploration).
6. **Duration:** 6 Days / 5 Nights total expedition.
7. **Official Portal:** `https://himalaya-escape.vercel.app` (or verified campus QR code).
8. **No False Scarcity:** We communicate real bus capacity (25 student passenger seats per coach), never fake countdown timers or manipulative scarcity.
