- **Brand:** HIMALAYAN ESCAPE
- **Campaign Identity:** THE GREAT ESCAPE
- **Main Tagline:** CAMPUS OFF. MOUNTAINS ON.
- **Supporting Tagline:** Six days away from routine. A lifetime of stories.
- **Destination:** MANALI × KASOL (with Solang Valley, Manikaran Sahib & Parvati Valley)
- **Duration:** 6 DAYS / 5 NIGHTS
- **Departure Point:** GLA University Campus, Mathura (Direct Pickup from Campus Gate)
- **Total Price:** Strictly **₹6,200 per person** (All-inclusive: luxury coach transit, verified mountain stays, meals, guided sightseeing)
- **Booking Deposit:** Strictly **₹1,000 per person** to lock seat today
- **Remaining Balance:** **₹5,200 per person**, payable before boarding as per confirmed payment schedule
- **Coach Architecture:** Strictly **25 student passenger seats** per coach + isolated front crew cabin for Driver & Conductor
- **Official Booking Portal:** `https://himalaya-escape.vercel.app` (Live seat reservation + Razorpay secure gateway + Instant digital QR boarding pass)
